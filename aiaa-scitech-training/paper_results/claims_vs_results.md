# Manuscript claims against the v2 results

For the authors, not text for the paper. Claims are paraphrased from NortheasternSatelliteLab_DroughtMonitoring.pdf (05/04/2026); numbers come from the same score files as the tables. AUC differences carry 95% intervals from 2,000 whole-region resamples.

Each finding leads with the plain Bi-LSTM, the paper's model. For job 1 it is not a pre-registered row: it was added to the single fresh-test scoring before any fresh-test label was read (DESIGN.md amendment 6). The Residual Bi-LSTM (best Bi-LSTM variant) and the GRU (best network) follow as job 1's pre-registered networks.

| Section | Claim | What the study found | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2 | A Bi-LSTM on HLS-derived NDVI/VCI, with FLDAS labels, beats simpler classifiers (logistic regression, decision trees) | Fresh test: Bi-LSTM − LR on VCI(T) +0.042 [+0.024, +0.060] (West +0.013 [−0.008, +0.035], East +0.037 [+0.010, +0.061]) and − boosted trees +0.042 [+0.024, +0.061]. For job 1 the Bi-LSTM is not a pre-registered row: it was added to the single fresh-test scoring before any fresh-test label was read (DESIGN.md amendment 6). The pre-registered rows all passed: Residual Bi-LSTM (best Bi-LSTM variant) − LR +0.038 [+0.020, +0.057] and − boosted trees +0.039 [+0.020, +0.057]; GRU (best network) − LR +0.035 [+0.019, +0.054] and − boosted trees +0.036 [+0.019, +0.054]. The three full-input networks are within 0.006 AUC of each other (Bi-LSTM − Residual Bi-LSTM +0.004 [−0.001, +0.009], Bi-LSTM − GRU +0.006 [+0.002, +0.011]). Development (4 folds): Bi-LSTM − LR −0.059 [−0.075, −0.043], Residual Bi-LSTM − LR −0.050 [−0.064, −0.035], both worse; every network trailed LR. The fresh period was drier: 39.5% of windows in drought against 23.0% of development windows. | Supported on the fresh test only |
| 2.2 | The network predicts a drought-stress probability | AUC measures ranking only. On the fresh test the probabilities run low: Bi-LSTM mean prediction 0.252 against an observed rate of 0.395, ECE 0.149, Brier 0.246 (the same three for the pre-registered networks: Residual Bi-LSTM 0.253, 0.150, 0.247; GRU 0.254, 0.149, 0.247). ECE of every model 0.149–0.168; Brier of LR 0.256, climatology 0.267. | Ranking supported; calibrated probabilities not |
| 2.2 | Calibrated against SPEI-12 as an independent benchmark | Scored after the lock against SPEI-12 drought (gridMET 1-year SPEI ≤ −0.84; 36.4% of 64,995 windows in drought, flags agree with FLDAS on 63.4%): LR on VCI(T) AUC 0.680 [0.645, 0.713]; Bi-LSTM − LR −0.035 [−0.057, −0.013]. Within each side the Bi-LSTM's difference is not distinguishable from zero (West −0.012 [−0.033, +0.009], East +0.025 [−0.012, +0.068]), so the overall gap comes mostly from ranking West windows against East ones. Pre-registered networks: Residual Bi-LSTM − LR −0.033 [−0.053, −0.011], GRU − LR −0.032 [−0.053, −0.012]. Against SPEI at 90, 180 and 270 days, Bi-LSTM − LR is +0.002 [−0.016, +0.021], −0.032 [−0.049, −0.013] and −0.024 [−0.042, −0.005]; against NLDAS-2 soil moisture the gain holds: +0.031 [+0.017, +0.044]. AUC tests ranking, not calibration. | Not supported: the gain reverses against SPEI-12 |
| 2.2 | Bidirectional processing improves on a conventional LSTM | Development, within-fold AUC: Bi-LSTM 0.627 [0.611, 0.644] against one-direction LSTM 0.641 [0.626, 0.658] (not a paired test); Residual Bi-LSTM 0.636 [0.620, 0.653]. The one-direction LSTM was not carried to the fresh test. | Not supported in development; not tested on the fresh test |
| 2.2 | A CNN spatial step on image tiles before the temporal model | CNN + Bi-LSTM − VCI-only Bi-LSTM: −0.008 [−0.016, +0.000] on the fresh test, +0.000 [−0.005, +0.005] in development. That comparison is against the VCI-only Bi-LSTM (both read VCI alone, as image tiles or as cell averages), not the paper's model. The full-input Bi-LSTM beats the CNN version by +0.027 [+0.013, +0.041] on the fresh test, with 80,688 multiply-adds per copy against 13,030,048. | Not supported |
| 2.2 | Distillation, pruning and INT8 quantization-aware training compress the model from about 1–2 MB to 30–100 KB with under 3% accuracy loss | One copy of the trained Bi-LSTM has 11,617 parameters and 49.5 KiB of float32 weights, already within 30–100 KB with no compression. Distillation, pruning and INT8 were not tested in this study (the earlier pipeline's distilled and INT8 models were trained on synthetic data). | Starting size doesn't match; compression not tested |
