# Manuscript claims against the v2 results

For the authors, not text for the paper. Claims are paraphrased from NortheasternSatelliteLab_DroughtMonitoring.pdf (05/04/2026); numbers come from the same score files as the tables. AUC differences carry 95% intervals from 2,000 whole-region resamples.

| Section | Claim | What the study found | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2 | A Bi-LSTM on HLS-derived NDVI/VCI, with FLDAS labels, beats simpler classifiers (logistic regression, decision trees) | Fresh test: Residual Bi-LSTM − LR on VCI(T) +0.038 [+0.020, +0.057] and − boosted trees +0.039 [+0.020, +0.057], both passed. Development (4 folds): − LR −0.050 [−0.064, −0.035], worse; every network trailed LR. The fresh period was drier: 39.5% of windows in drought against 22.1% in development. | Supported on the fresh test only |
| 2.2 | The network predicts a drought-stress probability | AUC measures ranking only. On the fresh test the probabilities run low: Residual Bi-LSTM mean prediction 0.253 against an observed rate of 0.395; ECE 0.150 (every model 0.149–0.168). Brier: Residual Bi-LSTM 0.247, LR 0.256, climatology 0.267. | Ranking supported; calibrated probabilities not |
| 2.2 | Calibrated against SPEI-12 as an independent benchmark | Scored after the lock against SPEI-12 drought (gridMET 1-year SPEI ≤ −0.84; 36.4% of 64,995 windows in drought, flags agree with FLDAS on 63.4%): LR on VCI(T) AUC 0.680 [0.645, 0.713]; Residual Bi-LSTM − LR −0.033 [−0.053, −0.011], GRU − LR −0.032 [−0.053, −0.012], Bi-LSTM − LR −0.035 [−0.057, −0.013]. Within each side the Residual Bi-LSTM's difference is not distinguishable from zero (West −0.011 [−0.032, +0.009], East +0.015 [−0.021, +0.055]), so the overall gap comes mostly from ranking West windows against East ones. Against SPEI at 90, 180 and 270 days, Residual Bi-LSTM − LR is −0.003 [−0.020, +0.014], −0.030 [−0.047, −0.012] and −0.021 [−0.038, −0.003]; against NLDAS-2 soil moisture the gain holds: +0.027 [+0.013, +0.041]. AUC tests ranking, not calibration. | Not supported: the gain reverses against SPEI-12 |
| 2.2 | Bidirectional processing improves on a conventional LSTM | Development, within-fold AUC: one-direction LSTM 0.641 [0.626, 0.658], Bi-LSTM 0.627 [0.611, 0.644], Residual Bi-LSTM 0.636 [0.620, 0.653] (not a paired test). The one-direction LSTM was not carried to the fresh test. | Not supported in development; not tested on the fresh test |
| 2.2 | A CNN spatial step on image tiles before the temporal model | CNN + Bi-LSTM − VCI-only Bi-LSTM: −0.008 [−0.016, +0.000] on the fresh test, +0.000 [−0.005, +0.005] in development. The full-input Bi-LSTM beats the CNN version by +0.027 [+0.013, +0.041], with 80,688 multiply-adds against 13,030,048. | Not supported |
| 2.2 | Distillation, pruning and INT8 quantization-aware training compress the model from about 1–2 MB to 30–100 KB with under 3% accuracy loss | The trained Bi-LSTM has 11,617 parameters and 49.5 KiB of float32 weights, already within 30–100 KB with no compression. Distillation, pruning and INT8 were not tested in this study (the earlier pipeline's distilled and INT8 models were trained on synthetic data). | Starting size doesn't match; compression not tested |
| 2.2 | Bi-LSTM and CNN inference in under 10 ms per tile on the NPU | Not measured on any flight processor. One Bi-LSTM prediction is 80,688 multiply-adds. | Not tested |
| 2.2 | Onboard triage gives up to 90% data reduction | Cited from reference [18]; not part of this study. | Not tested |
| 3.1 | The Bi-LSTM detects flash-drought anomalies earlier by modelling temporal change | Onset 1–3 months ahead (job 3), fresh test: Bi-LSTM AUC 0.538 [0.513, 0.565], LR + trend features 0.500 [0.473, 0.529]; Bi-LSTM − LR + trend +0.038 [+0.007, +0.069] passed, but development −0.038 [−0.054, −0.020], worse; AUC near 0.5 is near chance. Where VCI changed most (job 4), fresh test: Residual Bi-LSTM − LR +0.055 [+0.027, +0.081]; development − boosted trees −0.054 [−0.071, −0.037]. | Onset: not supported. Rapid change: fresh test only |
| 3.2 | A single CubeSat revisits every ≈16–40+ days | GRU − LR with one clear view every 7 days: +0.021 to +0.025 (3 of 3 start dates with the interval above zero); every 16 days: +0.005 to +0.023 (1 of 3 start dates with the interval above zero); every 32 days: −0.014 to +0.020 (0 of 3 start dates with the interval above zero). | The gain holds at 7 days and fades by 32 |
